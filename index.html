    About Password Generator

    Password Generator makes strong random passwords in your browser. Pick the length and the kinds of characters, check how strong it is, and copy it. It is a practice project by Zakria Khan (zakyprojects).

    How to use

    1. Pick a length and the characters you want.
    2. Make a new password and check its strength.
    3. Copy it and save it in your password manager.

    Questions

    Is it safe to use?

    The password is made in your browser with the Web Crypto random generator. It is never sent to a server.

    Are my passwords saved?

    No. They are cleared when you reload or close the page.

    How long should a password be?

    At least 14 characters, with a mix of letters, numbers and symbols, is a good start.
